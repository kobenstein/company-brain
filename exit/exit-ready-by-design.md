# Exit-Ready by Design

## CORE
Build a company that can be transferred without requiring a sale.

## First major optionality target
Approximately **€1.4–1.5M sale value** as a planning milestone.

Motivation: a possible path toward roughly €1M personal net wealth under a favorable future share-sale structure. Actual tax, fees and deal structure require professional advice at the time.

## Value drivers
- recurring revenue,
- retention,
- high margins,
- low customer concentration,
- low founder dependence,
- company-owned general IP,
- documented processes,
- clean account/data ownership,
- buyer-normalized economics,
- replaceable operators and tools.

No ARR multiple is guaranteed.
