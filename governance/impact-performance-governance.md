# Impact Performance Governance

## CORE
Impact integrity is a **constraint**, not a weighted KPI that better ROAS can offset.

### Gate 1 — Impact Integrity
A creative is eligible to scale only when:
- claims are truthful and supportable,
- material limitations are not hidden,
- no greenwashing or fabricated customer experience is used,
- promoted outcomes remain consistent with the customer’s genuine impact case.

### Gate 2 — Business Outcome
Among compliant creatives, optimize relevant outcomes such as:
CAC, qualified leads, purchases, contribution margin, conversion rate, ROAS/MER where appropriate.

### Layer 3 — Creative Diagnostics
Hook rate, watch time, CTR, hold rate and fatigue help explain performance. They are not ultimate outcomes.

### Layer 4 — Impact Evidence
Where practical, evaluate the intended positive action/outcome separately.
Do not infer long-term societal/environmental impact from short-term ad metrics.

## Governance principle
Performance expertise is powerful **inside** agreed impact boundaries.
