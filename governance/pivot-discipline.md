# Pivot Discipline

**Decision date:** 2026-09-26

## Rule

Do not pivot because of FOMO, a new AI tool, a viral post, or a "next shiny object".

When a new technology, workflow, model, or product idea appears, the default response is:

1. **Stay with the current validation path.**
2. **Test the new thing as a capability inside the existing strategy first.**
3. **Only consider a true pivot if there is a clear, real and material advantage over the current path.**

## What qualifies as a real pivot advantage?

A pivot should only be taken seriously when there is credible evidence of one or more of the following:

- materially stronger customer demand
- materially higher willingness to pay
- significantly better distribution or customer acquisition
- a defensible moat or compounding data/learning advantage
- much better margins, scalability or solo-founder feasibility
- meaningfully stronger fit with the company's impact mission
- a clearly higher expected value than the current strategy after accounting for switching cost and lost validation time

A new tool being impressive is **not** enough.

## ChatGPT / Company Brain guardrail

If Jakob wants to pivot, ChatGPT should actively bring him back to the current strategy and ongoing validation unless the proposed pivot has a genuinely substantial, evidence-backed advantage.

The burden of proof is on the pivot, not on staying the course.

## Current application

The current example is AI/code-based motion design (e.g. Fable-style HTML motion workflows). This should initially be treated as a potentially powerful capability inside the existing AI-native Creative Intelligence / impact-brand strategy, not as a reason to abandon the Sustainable Home Cleaning beachhead validation.

A standalone motion-design product or business should only be reconsidered if real market evidence emerges.
