# Sustainable / Impact Marketing

## Purpose
Keep growth communication aligned with truthful, useful and behaviorally realistic impact marketing.

## Knowledge track
- classical vs green vs social vs sustainability marketing,
- sustainable consumer behavior,
- attitude–behavior gap,
- segmentation and value propositions,
- impact positioning/storytelling,
- sustainable brand management,
- ethical communication,
- greenwashing / greenhushing,
- claims and evidence,
- campaign/media planning,
- performance marketing,
- impact measurement/reporting.

## Applied principle
Creative-performance learning can be fast; robust impact evaluation may require slower, different evidence.
