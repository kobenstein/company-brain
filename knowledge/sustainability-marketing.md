# Sustainable / Impact Marketing

Status: RESEARCH / Academy knowledge

## Curriculum

1.  Classical vs green vs social vs sustainability marketing
2.  Theory of Change / impact mapping
3.  Sustainable consumer behaviour: attitude--behaviour gap, barriers,
    norms, nudging
4.  Segmentation and value propositions
5.  Impact positioning and storytelling
6.  Sustainable brand management
7.  Ethics: greenwashing, greenhushing, claims/evidence, legal
    constraints
8.  Campaign/media planning
9.  Performance advertising
10. Impact measurement/reporting

## Frameworks to retain

Theory of Change; COM-B; SHIFT; Behaviour Change Technique thinking;
controlled testing.

## CORE derived principle

Fast creative/performance learning does not require months of individual
user tracking. Short-cycle performance learning and slower impact
validation are separate layers.
