# Behaviour Frameworks

## Theory of Change
Map activity → output → outcome → impact and make assumptions explicit.
An ad conversion is not itself proof of long-term impact.

## COM-B
Behavior requires sufficient Capability, Opportunity and Motivation.
Use it to diagnose whether adoption barriers are understanding/ability, access/friction, or motivation/trust/value.

## SHIFT
Use sustainability-behavior dimensions — social influence, habit, individual self, feelings/cognition, tangibility — as hypothesis generators, not guaranteed tactics.

## Behaviour Change Techniques
Use structured intervention concepts such as prompts, feedback, goal-setting, social support and environmental restructuring to describe the mechanism a creative is testing.

## Rule
Frameworks create testable hypotheses; customer evidence decides what works.
