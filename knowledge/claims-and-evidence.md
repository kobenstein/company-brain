# Claims & Evidence

Impact claims should be traceable to evidence appropriate for:
- exact product,
- geography,
- time period,
- lifecycle stage,
- exact wording.

Recommended claim register:
- claim,
- source/evidence,
- geography,
- validity period,
- limitations,
- responsible approver,
- last review date.

AI may identify unsupported claims, but legal/regulatory responsibility remains human.
