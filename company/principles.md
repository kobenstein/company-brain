# Principles

## CEO Simplicity — CORE
Reduce every recurring process to the smallest possible number of human decisions.

## CEO Exception Principle — CORE
Show the CEO only what requires judgment, approval or meaningful attention.

## Evidence before certainty — CORE
Ideas, hypotheses, decisions, learnings and validated knowledge are different states.

## Customer value — CORE
Aim for unusually strong value relative to price. Historical opportunity screening used a demanding 20–100× value aspiration.

## Measurable outcomes — CORE
Prefer value that can be observed through money, time, conversion, quality, risk or another concrete outcome.

## AI leverage — CORE
Revenue should grow much faster than human workload.

## Model robustness — CORE
Better AI models should strengthen the company rather than remove its reason to exist.

## Positive impact — CORE
Prefer models where company growth also increases real social/environmental value.

## Transferability — CORE
Knowledge, IP, accounts, processes and customer relationships must not exist only in the founder’s head or personal accounts.

## Automate observed repetition — CORE
Manual once → observe repetition → automate.
