# Goals

## Life/business design
Build a highly leveraged company run by one founder plus AI systems, with low operational complexity and eventually a sustainable 25–30 hour human work week.

## Near-term working targets
- paid customer validation quickly,
- recurring offer,
- approximately €10k MRR within ~6 months as an ambitious working target,
- reliable path to at least ~€4k/month private net income.

These are planning targets, not forecasts.

## Long-term optionality
Earlier ambition: €1–2M ARR.
Current sharper milestone: build an exit-ready company that could plausibly justify roughly **€1.4–1.5M sale value**.

Selling remains optional.
