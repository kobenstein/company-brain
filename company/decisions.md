- **2026-09-27 — Important company measure: account for and address the climate footprint of AI use.** Jakob wants the company to track its AI consumption across tools, including image and especially video generation, estimate associated CO₂e with transparent assumptions, and finance documented climate action/retired credits against the estimate. AI credits alone are not a reliable emissions unit; cross-provider automatic measurement is not yet established, and video estimates have material uncertainty. Do not claim that the company, AI outputs or credit usage are “CO₂-neutral” without defensible evidence and appropriate claims review. Status: company measure to implement; no tool selected, offset purchased or neutralization verified.
