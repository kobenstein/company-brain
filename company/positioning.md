# Positioning

## Current category hypothesis
**AI-native creative systems for impact-oriented companies.**

### Working category language — 2026-09-26
**Creative Intelligence Systems** is a strong working term for what the company is building.

Possible positioning language:
- **Creative Intelligence for Impact Brands**
- **We build Creative Intelligence Systems.**
- **Creative Intelligence Infrastructure for Impact Brands**

The term should describe a system that learns which creative decisions work for a specific brand and audience — not merely AI-generated content.

## Problem
Useful products can still fail to reach people because:
- value is not immediately understood,
- creative does not stop attention,
- messaging is poorly adapted to audience/context,
- production creates volume without learning,
- performance insights do not reliably return into the next brief.

## Differentiation hypothesis
Do not sell “more AI videos.”
Sell a **creative learning system / Creative Intelligence System** combining:
- high-end creative judgment,
- rapid generative production,
- performance feedback,
- brand/taste learning,
- impact integrity.

A core thesis is that exceptional creativity and performance are not opposites. The goal is to identify the strongest performance angle and express it through unusually strong creative concepts that make the message more memorable and more effective.

## Boundary
Do not claim to replace customer understanding, human judgment, legal claim review or the customer’s performance team before that is proven.
