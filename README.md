This repository is the durable, portable memory layer of the company.

## What belongs here
Only durable business knowledge: current strategy, decisions, hypotheses, evidence, customer/market learnings, reusable skills, governance and operating principles.

## Epistemic states
- **CORE** — foundational principle.
- **DECISION** — consciously chosen current direction.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — plausible but not yet validated.
- **RESEARCH** — external evidence or structured observation.
- **LEARNING** — insight derived from evidence or real work.
- **OPEN QUESTION** — unresolved.
- **ARCHIVED** — superseded or paused.

## Language policy
Canonical company knowledge is English. Original research, transcripts and source excerpts may remain in their original language under `research/source-material/`.
