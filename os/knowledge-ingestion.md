# Knowledge Ingestion Rules

Status: CORE

Goal: CEO should not manually file company knowledge.

Incoming items classify as: ephemeral/ignore; task; hypothesis;
decision; customer knowledge; reusable company learning; research; core
strategy change.

## Trust levels

1.  Raw/source material --- automatic capture allowed.
2.  Extracted learning --- AI may propose/store with source, date and
    confidence.
3.  Core truths/strategy --- explicit human approval before changing.

## Evidence progression

IDEA → HYPOTHESIS → TESTING → VALIDATED → CORE KNOWLEDGE

Potential sources: ChatGPT, customer email, Meet transcripts,
performance data, client feedback, research, internal communication.

Never let one conversation/campaign silently rewrite core strategy.
