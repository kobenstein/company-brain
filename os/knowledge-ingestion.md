# Knowledge Ingestion

Goal: the CEO should not manually organize company knowledge.

Incoming information becomes:
- ignore / ephemeral,
- task,
- research,
- hypothesis,
- decision,
- customer knowledge,
- reusable learning,
- core-strategy change.

## Trust levels
1. raw evidence can be captured automatically,
2. extracted learning may be stored with source/date/confidence,
3. core strategy/governance requires explicit human approval.

Progression:
**IDEA → HYPOTHESIS → TESTING → VALIDATED → CORE**

One call, email or campaign result must never silently rewrite company truth.
