# Account & Data Ownership

Track every company service by:
- service,
- purpose,
- owner entity,
- billing entity,
- admin identity,
- payment method,
- recovery/2FA,
- customer data involved?,
- transferable on exit?,
- migration notes.

## CORE
General company methods/skills/SOPs should be company-owned.
Customer-specific confidential data, brand assets and performance history remain clearly separated and governed by permission/contract.

A future buyer must be able to understand what is owned, licensed, customer-owned, transferable or deletion-bound.
