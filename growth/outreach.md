# Outreach

Early outreach is both market research and sales.

## Initial flow
1. research a relevant impact brand,
2. inspect product, positioning and public creative/performance signals,
3. form a specific hypothesis,
4. send a highly personalized message,
5. show a concrete idea when justified,
6. use calls to learn real pain and buyer language.

## Customer discovery call lens
When preparing or running customer calls, explicitly investigate **both** sides of AI-native value creation:

1. **Cost / labor replacement** — Where does the company currently spend substantial employee, freelancer, agency, coordination or production time and money? Quantify roles, hours, external spend, bottlenecks and delays where possible.
2. **Capability expansion** — What would the company like to do creatively that it cannot currently do because of limited time, budget, team size, coordination capacity or learning speed?

The goal is not only to automate existing work more cheaply. Prefer opportunities where the system can also create a qualitatively new capability that a conventional human team could not economically operate — for example, far more meaningful creative experiments, systematic tagging and learning, faster feedback loops, or continuously improved next-best-test decisions.

Key discovery questions:
- Where is the most creative work, coordination and cost today?
- What would you do if time, budget and team size were not constraints?
- Which creative activities do you know you *should* be doing, but currently cannot do consistently?
- Where would 10x more experimentation or learning speed materially improve the business?

Strategic principle: **The strongest AI-native value proposition combines labor compression, better decisions and new capabilities — not cost reduction alone.**

## Working cadence
Prefer a small number of high-quality contacts over premature mass automation.

## Evidence hierarchy
qualified reply → discovery call → pilot discussion → paid pilot → recurring contract.

Do not automate outreach before a repeatable ICP/message pattern exists.
