# Outreach

Status: HYPOTHESIS / validation loop

Early outreach = market research + sales.

Initial approach: - identify relevant impact companies - inspect
product/positioning/current ads - form a specific creative/performance
hypothesis - send highly personalized outreach - show rather than merely
claim creative capability - use discovery calls to learn market language
and pain

Working cadence hypothesis: \~5 high-quality contacts/day.

Early call role test: Jakob = creative/brand/impact. Pascal =
performance/spend/CAC/testing/conversion/bottlenecks.

## DECISION

Do not automate outreach before learning which outreach works.
