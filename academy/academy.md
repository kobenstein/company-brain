# AI Business Academy

Purpose: learn by building the real company.

Tracks:
- customer/problem discovery,
- AI-native company design,
- creative systems,
- performance marketing,
- sustainable/impact marketing,
- sales/outreach,
- economics/finance,
- automation/agents,
- transferability / exit-readiness.

Learning follows the current business bottleneck.
Avoid courses disconnected from live decisions.

Early planning used ~25 hours/week initially and ~30–35 hours later.
Long-term aim: lower founder operating load.
