# Skill — Impact Creative Brief

Inputs:
- product / offer,
- audience,
- behavioral barrier,
- brand/taste context,
- evidenced claims,
- prohibited/risky claims,
- business objective,
- campaign context,
- existing learnings.

Output:
- customer problem,
- creative hypothesis,
- proposed mechanism,
- concept,
- first 1–3 second hook,
- proof/demo,
- CTA,
- impact-integrity check,
- validation/falsification signal.
