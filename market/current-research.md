# Current Market Research

## Latest structured scan
73 impact-oriented brands reviewed:
- 66 candidates,
- 7 held/unresolved.

This is **not confirmed buying intent or budget**.

## Current initial-ICP hypothesis
Repeat-purchase brands with existing performance marketing, especially:
- refill / household,
- personal care,
- selected recurring-consumption impact categories.

## P1 research examples
Fussy, smol, SURI, everdrop, Blueland, Who Gives A Crap, Tru Earth, B.O.B / Bars Over Bottles, Oddbox.

## Key findings
Target companies publicly show concern for:
- creative testing,
- profitability/customer economics,
- localization,
- authenticity,
- operational production quality,
- impact-claim credibility.

## Competition
Existing agencies and in-house teams already provide creative testing systems.
The opportunity is **not yet proven to be a Blue Ocean**.
