# Customer Language Themes

Paraphrased themes from public research:
- profitable customers matter more than reach alone,
- creative output should improve the next test,
- localization must land culturally,
- authenticity and brand fit matter,
- approvals/rights/formats are real operational constraints,
- creator/media activity should connect to customer economics,
- repeat purchase and customer value matter,
- different channels serve different roles,
- mission does not remove convenience barriers,
- impact claims need evidence.

These are research themes, not universal truths.
