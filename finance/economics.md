# Economics

Track from the first customer:
- MRR / recurring revenue,
- one-off revenue separately,
- direct AI/API/production cost,
- contribution margin,
- founder hours,
- acquisition cost/time,
- customer concentration,
- invoiced vs cash collected.

For exit-readiness also maintain:
- adjusted EBITDA,
- founder replacement cost,
- extraordinary/non-recurring costs.

~€5k/month remains a working price hypothesis, not proven pricing.
