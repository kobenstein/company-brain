# Risk Register

## Market
- impact brands may not have enough recurring budget,
- in-house teams may prefer internal capability,
- €5k/month may be too high initially,
- campaign volume may be too low for fast learning.

## Product
- model progress commoditizes production,
- attribution/performance interpretation is noisy,
- customer-specific work creates hidden agency complexity.

## Impact
- weak/incorrect claims,
- more sales do not automatically mean net-positive impact,
- performance optimization rewards manipulative creative without guardrails.

## Organization
- founder dependency,
- unclear Pascal incentives,
- fragmented knowledge,
- tool/automation maintenance burden.

## Exit
- customer concentration,
- perpetual partner revenue shares,
- unclear IP/account ownership,
- customer data not transferable.
