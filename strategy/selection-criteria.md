# Opportunity Selection Criteria

## Six hard gates
1. Very little direct competition / meaningfully new category or workflow.
2. Plausible 20–100× customer value relative to price.
3. Measurable ROI or customer outcome.
4. Credible path to €1–2M recurring/repeatable annual revenue.
5. Scaling primarily through AI/software rather than headcount.
6. Fast real-world validation.

## Additional preferred properties
- self-improving feedback loop,
- proprietary outcome data,
- stronger as models improve,
- low human work per customer,
- solo-founder compatibility,
- fast technical feasibility,
- fast path to revenue,
- recurring revenue,
- high gross margins,
- narrow initial ICP + expansion potential,
- positive impact that grows with revenue,
- founder advantage,
- low operational complexity,
- outcome ownership,
- acquisition potential,
- global scalability,
- rising difficulty to copy.

## Rule
“Open” is not “validated.”
