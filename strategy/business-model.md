# Business Model

## Current hypothesis
High-value recurring B2B creative system/service, initially around paid-social video creative.

## Why recurring
Repeated campaigns create:
- repeated tests,
- accumulated customer knowledge,
- performance learning,
- workflow integration,
- predictable revenue.

## Price
~€5,000/month is a mature-customer working hypothesis.
It is **not validated willingness-to-pay**.

## Economic design
Prefer variable AI/tool costs and high leverage over employee-heavy delivery.
Track contribution margin and founder time per customer immediately.

## Avoid
A disguised agency where each new customer requires proportional founder production.
