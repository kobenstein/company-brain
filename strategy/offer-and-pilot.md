# Offer & Pilot

## Pilot principle
One customer, one product area, one market, one existing channel, a small number of explicit hypotheses.

Do not simultaneously rebuild ads, landing page, CRM and attribution.

## Measure
- production time,
- direct cost,
- manual interventions,
- approval/rejection,
- campaign performance and uncertainty,
- whether learning improves the next creative round,
- operational/decision relief for the customer.

## Success
Not “better-looking creative.”
A successful pilot reveals a repeatable economic or operational reason to continue.

## HYPOTHESIS
Paid pilots may create stronger evidence than free work because willingness to pay is itself a signal.
