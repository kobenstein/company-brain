# Agentic Market Thesis

Do not build only on mature, crowded internet roles.
Look for new economic roles created by agentic computing, analogous to early YouTube creators or social-media influencers.

Desired properties:
- early-entry advantage,
- compounding distribution/data,
- agent-native workflows,
- value that grows with AI capability,
- ownership of workflow/data/distribution rather than simple model access.

The company itself should be agent-native, but “uses agents” is not differentiation by itself.
