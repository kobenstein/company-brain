# Creative Learning Loop

**customer context → angle → hook → concept → creative execution → campaign → data → interpretation → reusable learning → next iteration**

## Creative hierarchy
- **Angle** = what reason/perspective makes the product relevant to the audience.
- **Hook** = how attention is captured immediately.
- **Concept** = the creative idea or metaphor that carries the message.
- **Format / execution** = how the idea is expressed (UGC, cinematic, animation, surreal visual, etc.).
- **Offer / CTA** = what the audience is asked to do or receive.

The system should learn at each level rather than merely comparing finished videos.

Example long-term learning:
> “For sustainable home-care brands, a health/family angle outperforms a generic environmental angle for cold traffic.”

That kind of reusable creative knowledge is a potential compounding asset.

## Two separate learning streams

### Customer Taste
What the brand approves, rejects and considers on-brand.

### Market Performance
What produces useful business outcomes.

Never silently equate them.

The long-term asset is the accumulated decision system — the **Creative Intelligence** — not the raw number of generated videos.
