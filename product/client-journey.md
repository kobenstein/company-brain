# Client Journey

1. qualification,
2. discovery: business + impact + claim boundaries,
3. brand/assets/data access,
4. test plan,
5. creative production,
6. approval,
7. campaign launch by agreed owner,
8. performance interpretation,
9. learning extraction,
10. next iteration.

Default client communication: email + Google Meet.

A client portal is a future option only if review/versioning becomes a repeated pain.
