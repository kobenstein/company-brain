# Pascal — Collaboration Hypothesis

## Known
Pascal brings performance-marketing knowledge and has participated in strategy calls.
Historically, a 25% unlimited revenue share applied to customers he brought.

## Changed context
The target market/product is changing, so the old structure may no longer match contribution.

## Potential ownership
- performance strategy,
- testing methodology,
- media buying,
- client performance calls,
- campaign interpretation,
- training/documenting reusable performance knowledge,
- sales for leads he genuinely sources.

## OPEN QUESTION
External partner, key operator, strategic partner, or true cofounder?

## Principle
Define durable ownership/contribution first, then compensation.

Do not grant broad permanent revenue/equity rights before role and commitment are clear.
